# Senior Citizen Secure Pension Scheme (SCSP)

> IMPORTANT: This is a fictional demonstration policy created for a software
> prototype. It is not an actual government pension scheme.

## 1. Overview

The Senior Citizen Secure Pension Scheme (SCSP) is a fictional monthly
pension program created to demonstrate a voice-assisted policy helper for
elderly and retired citizens.

## 2. Eligibility

A person can apply if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

- Applicant must be a resident of India.
- Applicant must be at least 60 years old.
- Applicant's annual household income must be below ₹3,00,000.
- Applicant must not already receive a monthly pension greater than ₹10,000
  from another scheme.

## 3. Pension Amount

- Age 60–69: ₹2,000 per month
- Age 70–79: ₹2,500 per month
- Age 80 and above: ₹3,000 per month

Payments are made directly to the beneficiary's registered bank account.

## 4. Additional Senior Support

Beneficiaries aged 75 and above can request:

- Assisted application support
- Annual document verification assistance
- Priority grievance handling

## 5. Required Documents

1. Aadhaar card or another accepted identity document
2. Age proof
3. Address proof
4. Bank account details
5. Income certificate
6. Recent photograph

## 6. Application Process

1. Complete the application form.
2. Submit identity and age documents.
3. Submit income certificate.
4. Provide bank account details.
5. Application is reviewed.
6. Eligible applications are approved.
7. Pension payments begin from the following payment cycle.

## 7. Application Status Values

- Submitted
- Under Verification
- Approved
- Rejected
- Payment Started
- Additional Documents Required

## 8. Rejection Reasons

- Applicant is below 60 years old.
- Household income exceeds ₹3,00,000.
- Required documents are missing.
- Submitted information cannot be verified.
- Applicant already receives a conflicting pension benefit.

## 9. Grievances

A beneficiary can raise a grievance if:

- Pension payment is delayed.
- Pension amount is incorrect.
- Application has been pending for more than 30 days.
- Personal information is incorrect.
- Bank account details need correction.

A grievance record needs: applicant name, application ID, phone number, and
a description of the problem.

## 10. Assistant Rules

- Speak slowly and clearly, in simple language.
- Explain one thing at a time.
- Repeat information when asked.
- Answer only using this policy document — never invent eligibility rules,
  amounts, or documents.
- Clearly say when information is not available in this document.
- If asked whether SCSP is a real government scheme, say it is a fictional
  demonstration policy.
- When eligibility depends on age or income, ask the user for those details
  if not already given.
